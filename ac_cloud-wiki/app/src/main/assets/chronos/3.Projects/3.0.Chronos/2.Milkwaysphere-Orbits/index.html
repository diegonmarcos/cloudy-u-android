1.0 Planetary & Moon Orbits

3D Simulation

Full Keplerian orbital simulation with planetary trajectories and individual moon subsystem revolutions.

• Sun & Rocky Worlds: Mercury, Venus, Earth with Moon, and Mars with Phobos and Deimos.

• Gas Giants: Jupiter with its four Galilean satellites (Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto).

• Interactive orbit rings demonstrate relative distances and orbital speeds.

1.1 Constellations & Celestial Sphere

3D Sphere

3D interactive celestial sphere displaying major stellar asterisms, ecliptic tilt plane, and equatorial coordinate grids.

• Highlighted Asterisms: Orion, Ursa Major (Big Dipper), Cassiopeia, Taurus, and Scorpius.

• Ecliptic Coordinate Ring: Traces the annual path of the Sun across the background stars.

1.2 Astrological & Galactic Magnetic Flux

3D Vector Field

Dynamic 3D vector ribbons modeling the Parker spiral, solar wind plasma sheets, and interstellar cosmic rays.

• Parker Spiral Streamers: Archimedean magnetic flux lines carried into interplanetary space by solar rotation.

• Heliospheric Wave: Animated wavy current sheet modulating incoming galactic cosmic radiation.

2. Andromeda Galaxy (M31)

14k Particle Disk

3D volumetric N-body particle simulation of our closest giant spiral galaxy neighbor (Messier 31) with satellite galaxies M32 and M110.

• Distance & Scale: 2.5 million light-years, spanning ~220,000 light-years across.

• Features dense supermassive galactic bulge, dust lanes, and spiral star-forming arms.

3. Triangulum Galaxy (M33)

10k Particle Cloud

3D simulation of the third-largest member of the Local Group, showing flocculent unbarred spiral arms and giant starburst nebulae.

• Distance: ~2.73 million light-years in the constellation Triangulum.

• Highlights bright magenta H-II star-forming clusters including NGC 604.
