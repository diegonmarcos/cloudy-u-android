  http://www.muldjord.com/). Stereo edition assembled by Roberto (roberto@zenvoid.org) for the FreePats project.

  archive https://github.com/freepats/muldjordkit/releases/download/2020-10-18/MuldjordKit-20201018.h2drumkit
  (the FreePats project's release of version 2020-10-18).
* **Attribution shown in the app**: Configs > About > Credits, and the line under the Play instrument chips.

## What was changed (#1787, Cloud Canvas)

Nine kit pieces, three velocity layers each (the hits at 30 %, 65 % and 95 % of the piece's layer list, softest first),
27 files. Each was mixed to mono (L+R average), cut (hats closed 0.8 s, kick and snare 1.5 s, toms 1.8 to 2 s, hats open
2.5 s, crash and ride 4 s) with a fade over the last 40 %, and encoded as Ogg Vorbis (quality 5, 44.1 kHz). Encoder:
ffmpeg/libvorbis. The Clap pad has no acoustic counterpart in the kit and stays synthesized.
